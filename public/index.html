Wave periods

Seconds, sharing one axis. Names ending in “, buoy” are as transmitted; the rest are computed here from the spectrum, through the taper. Tp is 1/f at the spectral peak, Tm01 = m₀/m₁, Tm02 = √(m₀/m₂). Two lines are the same quantity twice — Tp against Tp, buoy, and Tm02 against Tz — and the gap in each pair is what the taper and the wire format removed. Tp steps in whole bins, so read trends off Tm01 and Tm02.

Spectrum waterfall

Elevation PSD, m²/Hz, derived from the transmitted acceleration PSD. One column per measurement, evenly spaced — hover a column to read it below; click to pin it. Bins the taper in the filter row zeroes are not drawn, and the rest are weighted by it.
